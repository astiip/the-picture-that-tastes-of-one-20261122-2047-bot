A single molten chocolate lava cake, cut open, thick glossy chocolate flowing out of its centre and pooling on a dark plate, a faint wisp of steam rising. One ingredient, chocolate, is the whole picture: the cake fills the frame in a tight close-up, warm side light catching the molten gloss, so one look is enough to taste it — and at ~300px across the dish is still clear and delicious.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) copies the bundled `entry.png` to `(process.env.MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR ?? '/output')/entry.png`. The image is a newly generated photograph-style picture shipped in this repository; the program does no network access and no generation at run time.
